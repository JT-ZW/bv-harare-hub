# Design direction

The audience is potential collaborators, customers, mentors, and the Harare chapter community. The main action is discovering a fellow and following an available professional contact link. The noticeboard becomes the second discovery route as chapter content is published.

The site uses the global fellowship's cream, black, yellow, lilac, turquoise, and orange palette, bold Articulat headings, editorial spacing, rounded image corners, circular arrows, and prominent photography. The Harare identity sits alongside the global wordmark. Illustrative initials keep the directory useful without misrepresenting people through stock portraits.

Reference pages reviewed: global homepage, About, Our Fellows, Insights & Stories. Public HTML and global stylesheet were inspected for typography, spacing, colour tokens, navigation, profile/story structure, imagery, and footer patterns. This chapter adapts those patterns to a locally focused directory and noticeboard rather than copying global application content.

Responsive behavior: three directory columns on wide screens; one on narrow phones. Impact-zone tiles become two columns. Desktop navigation becomes a menu. Controls remain labelled; focus rings, skip navigation, result announcements, and reduced-motion handling are included.

Content model: a fellow can work in several impact zones and participate in projects; a project can have several fellows; products/services are separate offerings; notices can link to fellows and offerings. The leadership controls publication. No fellow sign-in is needed in the public release.

October 27 refinement: added a compact global-wordmark/Harare badge lockup. The yellow word strip now uses four evenly spaced words with symmetric padding, and a two-column layout on phones.
